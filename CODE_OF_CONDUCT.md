# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Gitma a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of positive behavior include showing empathy, respecting differing
viewpoints, giving constructive feedback, and accepting responsibility.
Harassment, personal attacks, trolling, discriminatory language, and publishing
someone else's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through GitHub's repository contact
options. Maintainers may remove or reject comments, commits, issues, and other
contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban a
contributor for repeated or serious violations.

This code is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html
